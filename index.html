Commercial & Settlement Framework

How Daily Ride Bazar Operates

Designed for transparent merchant onboarding, direct customer-to-shop payments, and automated fee collection.

01

Merchant Onboarding & Storefront

Shopkeepers register their business profile, category, city, and direct payment methods (Bank Transfer, Easypaisa, JazzCash, or COD) on the platform.

02

Direct Customer-to-Merchant Payment

Customers place orders directly with the merchant. 100% of the order value is settled directly into the shopkeeper's own designated account without intermediary holding.

03

5% Platform Fee via 1BILL Token

Upon completed orders, a 5% platform service fee is generated against a unique merchant Token ID, payable via 1LINK / 1BILL banking channels within 24 hours.
